Dino Ranch

Animated Television Series
Writer · 2 Episodes
▶

Jane's Perfect Present

After the Ranchers spend all day trying to prepare a special surprise birthday gift for Jane, they find out that all she really wanted was to spend it with them, and that spending time with someone is the best gift of all.

Writer's Note

The inspiration for this episode came from wanting to send the kids on a fun chase for an item that kept getting tossed from dino to dino to dino.

▶

Tango's BFF

After helping a fellow triceratops, Tango becomes attached to her new friend, but learns it's selfish to keep her from her herd.

Writer's Note

This story was inspired by the "if you love something let it go" adage, which I think is a tough message for kids to understand, so using wild animals as the focus helped to bridge that concept.

Writer · 4 Episodes

Gary's Cousin Conundrum

When Gary's cousin playdate gets cancelled, Gary tries to recreate the day without him, but he can't seem to get it right. Gary's friends help him learn that changing your plans is okay too!

Writer's Note

I loved the idea of Gary being delusiona and 'seeing' his cousin Gregary everywhere. The cousin was originally going to be played by the life-sized mascot, which would have been a funy trippy visual.

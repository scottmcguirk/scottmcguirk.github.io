Great X-Mentations

Building a Niche Audience

I created Great X-Mentations after identifying underserved niches within X-Men fandom. I'm a huge fan, and wanted to bring my expertise to the masses! My goal was to monetize quickly & efficiently with integrity, and I accomplished just that. I developed distinct editorial formats around costume ranking, character deep-dives, and rapid-response comic news. Then I researched, wrote, recorded, and edited every video myself.

700K+ Lifetime Views
95K+ Hours Watched
< 3 Months To Monetization
Breakout Format

Outfit Rankings

A costume-ranking format built around fashion, character history, and fan opinion. Six videos generated 277K+ views, and more than half of the channel’s lifetime watch time. It was very evident that this was my fan-favourite format, and as no-one else was really doing it, I had the market cornered.

Editorial Strategy

First to Story

Rapid-response coverage turned breaking X-Men news into timely editorial content. The format became a powerful audience-growth driver, with the channel’s top subscriber-converting video generating 35K+ views and 400+ new subscribers. This style of video was my main strategy to meet the monetization thresholds, and the key of its success was truly having to be FIRST to market with the story, which was challenging to do given I was a one-man-show up against powerfully scaled fandom organizations.

Finding Underserved Audiences

Going Deeper

I deliberately looked beyond Marvel's biggest names to find characters, stories, and corners of X-Men history that were underserved on YouTube. Character spotlights and long-form comic reviews turned niche subjects into discoverable content, with multiple deep-dives reaching audiences in the tens of thousands. My little corner of the X-Men YouTube fandom was all about celebrating the b-list character, knowing that my strength was in niche creation vs mainstream popularity.

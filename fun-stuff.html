03 / FUN STUFF

Drag + Performance

Part stage show, part creative laboratory. These projects bring together live entertainment, fundraising, event marketing and original music videos.

LIVE ENTERTAINMENT · COMMUNITY FUNDRAISING

Live Pageants &
Fundraising

What happens when you combine drag queens, football players, and a whole lot of ambition? A fabulous fundraiser, apparently! I handled every aspect of producing these annual drag pageants—from coordinating performers and planning the show to designing promotional materials, creating videos, hosting, and performing. Across pageants I’ve organized and participated in, performer tips have collectively raised approximately $50,000 for the football league. Not bad for a one-night community event!

Concept · Event Production · Creative Direction · Promotion · Hosting · Performance

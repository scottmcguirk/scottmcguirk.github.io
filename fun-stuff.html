03 / FUN STUFF

Performances + Fundraising

Part stage show, part creative laboratory. These projects bring together live entertainment, fundraising, event marketing, and original music videos.

TORONTO UNITED FLAG FOOTBALL

Live Performances

The Mx TUFF Drag Pageant is an annual fundraiser for the Toronto United Flag Football League. After participating in its very bare-bones inaugural 2015 year (and eventually snatching the crown in 2018), I took the lead on producing the entire show, helping transform it into the premier summer event of the football season. It has since become the league's biggest & now only fundraiser, raising a cumulative $50,000 to date.

I oversee every aspect of production - from coordinating performers, venues, and staff to designing promotional materials and props, managing budgets and logistics, and, of course, hosting and performing myself. It's a show I'm incredibly proud of, and one that brings our community together in a truly unique way.

ORIGINAL CONTENT

Digital Drag

During COVID lockdowns, we kept our community (and our fans!) entertained by creating wild, campy music videos featuring our drag personas. Whether filmed together or remotely, each production started with a clear creative vision and detailed storyboards. I directed performers on framing, lighting, and filming their own footage, then brought everything together in the edit to create something delightfully draggy. And people are still watching them today!

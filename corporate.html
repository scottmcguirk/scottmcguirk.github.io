TVO

Black Student Summer Leadership Program

An annual five-week program that engages student leaders in an immersive internship across all TVO departments. My role follows the cohort throughout the program, capturing their unfiltered experiences on camera, and producing a capstone graduation video that celebrates their growth. The goal is to showcase the breadth of fun & learning opportunities offered by the program, as well as to trigger some happy tears if we can :)
